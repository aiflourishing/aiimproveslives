# Goals

Build one clear, useful place where anyone can find examples of AI improving people's lives. Include any kind of AI, across fields and countries.

Make examples easy to understand, check, and share. Describe what changed for people, how AI contributed, and what the evidence can actually support.

Let the community contribute and correct examples. A moderator team should apply shared criteria so the collection does not depend on one person's judgment. Publication requires human review.

Over time, make work that improves lives more visible and worth pursuing. Let the examples and the credit given to useful work express this goal; keep it out of the site's pitch.

Build a resource that can inform public conversations and, eventually, policy. The collection documents benefits; it does not establish AI's overall balance of benefits and harms.

Launch a small, compelling version within a week. Keep the website and contribution process simple enough for volunteers to maintain.
