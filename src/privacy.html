Privacy

AI Improves Lives is a public, community-maintained collection. You can browse without an account. Signing in is required to contribute or react.

Sign-in and reactions

Supabase handles sign-in through Google or GitHub. It receives your account identifier, email address, and basic profile information from the provider. We use that information to authenticate you, associate your contributions and reactions with your account, and limit abuse. The site does not request access to your Google documents, email messages, or GitHub repositories.

Website reactions are stored privately with your account identifier and timestamps. Other visitors cannot retrieve your individual website reactions. The public Top ordering combines website reactions with reactions on the original GitHub review. GitHub reactions, their authors, and review discussions are public on GitHub.

Contributions

Submitting an entry publishes the fields you entered in a GitHub Issue for review. Accepted entries also appear in the repository and on this site. Do not include private information in your submission. Your sign-in email and account identifier are not added to the Issue by the contribution form. Private submission receipts connect your account to the Issue and prevent duplicate submissions.

Your browser and service providers

Your browser stores your sign-in session and unfinished contribution drafts. An accepted submission clears its saved draft. GitHub hosts the website and public records; Supabase provides authentication, private storage, and the submission service. These providers process requests under their own privacy policies. Images supplied with entries load from their source websites, which receive the image requests.

Your choices

You can remove your website reaction by clicking it again while signed in. Clear this site's browser data to remove local drafts and sessions. Public contributions may remain in GitHub history after an edit or removal. For questions about your account or stored data, contact shashwatnow@gmail.com.
