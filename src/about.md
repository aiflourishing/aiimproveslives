# Has AI Improved Lives?

AI discourse recently has been about how “intelligent” the models are, what jobs they might automate, the trillions being invested, and who will win the “AI race”.  This understandably worries people, and makes them averse to AI.

As a field, we're missing the point. The goal of technology should be to improve people's lives. We don't need to speculate about the grand challenges AI will solve for us, when there are systems deployed today that already benefit people.

**This is the ultimate evaluation for AI.**
