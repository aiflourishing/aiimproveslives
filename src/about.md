# Has AI Improved Lives?

Much debate around AI has focused on how “intelligent” the models are, what jobs they might automate, and who will win the “AI race”. We're all missing the point.

The goal of technology should be to improve people's lives. We don't need to rely on promises such as “one day AI will help cure cancer”, when right now, there are systems deployed that are helping people.

**This is the ultimate evaluation for AI.**
